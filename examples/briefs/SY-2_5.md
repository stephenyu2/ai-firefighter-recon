- Thermal data confirms the presence of high temperatures (up to 187.4 C) in the first six frames.
- Frame 8 shows the same general area, but the blackened patch and smoke are no longer visible.

**Inferences**

- A fire was active in the area shown in the first frame.
- By the time of the last frame, the fire was either extinguished or had moved out of the camera's view.
